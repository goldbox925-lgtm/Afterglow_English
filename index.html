01 Numbness
one window flickers faintly in the distance. Find it and click it. Hints appear if you need them.
02 Fracture
Click four glowing windows. Their light will stay for good.
03 Awakening
Click five dark buildings marked with a glowing ring. The light will pass to them.
04 Reclaiming Yourself
Connect five pairs of golden points on the buildings. Drag between them or click one, then the other.
05 Rebirth
steer the flight: mouse or ← → to turn, ↓ to descend, tap ↑ for a small lift, double-tap ↑ to rise, hold ↑ to speed up. Collect all five fragments to enter the light.
Audio reactivity
with AUDIO REACTIVITY ON, the soundtrack's bass swells the city glow, its mids pulse the windows and its highs make them sparkle. OFF returns the original, steady look; the music keeps playing.
