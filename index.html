01 Numbness
one window flickers faintly in the distance. Find it and click it. Hints appear if you need them.
02 Fracture
Click four glowing windows. Their light will stay for good.
03 Awakening
Click five dark buildings marked with a glowing ring. The light will pass to them.
04 Reclaiming Yourself
Connect five pairs of golden points on the buildings. Drag between them or click one, then the other.
05 Rebirth
steer the flight: mouse or ← → to turn, ↓ to descend, hold ↑ to rise quickly toward the light.
Microphone
